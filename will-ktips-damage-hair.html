Will K-Tips make your hair fall out?

K-Tip extensions are a professional hair service. Hair condition, placement, installation, maintenance and removal all matter.

Start with a consultation.

A consultation helps determine whether K-Tips are appropriate for your hair and what placement and maintenance plan makes sense.

Maintenance matters.

Following professional care instructions can help reduce unnecessary stress on the natural hair.

Professional removal matters too.

K-Tips should not be pulled or forced out. Proper removal is an important part of protecting the natural hair.

General hair-care information; not medical advice.
